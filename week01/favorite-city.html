Ilha Bela: My Favorite City

Ilha Bela is a stunning island located in the state of São Paulo, Brazil. Known for its pristine beaches, lush rainforests, and crystal-clear waters, it offers a perfect escape from urban life. The island is part of the Serra do Mar State Park, making it a haven for nature lovers and adventurers alike.

Visitors can enjoy activities like hiking, snorkeling, and exploring historic sites such as the old fortresses. With its rich biodiversity and tranquil atmosphere, Ilha Bela truly captures the essence of Brazil's coastal beauty.
